# AkerDock Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want AkerDock to be a welcoming, safe, and productive project for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability. Everyone who takes
part in the project is expected to help maintain that environment.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- communicating with empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving specific, constructive technical feedback;
- welcoming questions and different levels of experience;
- acknowledging mistakes and helping repair their impact;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users; and
- respecting privacy, consent, and responsible disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- discriminatory language, imagery, jokes, or unwanted sexual attention;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- exploiting project channels for spam or unrelated promotion; or
- any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

Disagreement about code, architecture, or product direction is normal. It must
remain about the work rather than the people doing it.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, release channels, and project-related events. It also
applies when someone officially represents AkerDock in another public space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct incidents privately to the maintainers at
[contact@deepteams.io](mailto:contact@deepteams.io). When the incident concerns
content on GitHub, you may also use the **Report content** action on that item.
Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.

Include links or screenshots, relevant dates, and any context that can help the
maintainers understand the incident. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as reasonably possible. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and will
not retaliate against a good-faith reporter.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code.
Depending on the severity, impact, and history of the behavior, they may issue
a private warning, limit participation, temporarily suspend participation, or
permanently ban a contributor from project spaces. A credible threat or illegal
conduct may also be reported to the appropriate platform or authorities.

Maintainers who do not apply or enforce this code in good faith may be removed
from their project responsibilities.
